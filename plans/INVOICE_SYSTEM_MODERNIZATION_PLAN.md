## 🎯 **Invoice System Requirements**

### **Must Support Three Billing Models:**

#### **1. Project-Based Pricing** (Websites, SEO Audits)
- Fixed-price packages (e.g., "Professional Website Package - $2,500")
- Milestone-based billing (e.g., "50% deposit, 50% on completion")
- Add-ons and upgrades

#### **2. Hourly Billing** (IT Support, Consulting, Custom Development)
- Variable hourly rates by service type ($80-175/hour)
- Accurate time tracking (quarter-hour increments)
- Travel time and on-site fees

#### **3. Monthly Retainers** (SEO Plans, Managed IT, Website Maintenance)
- Recurring monthly invoices
- Service level tracking
- Prorated billing for mid-month starts
