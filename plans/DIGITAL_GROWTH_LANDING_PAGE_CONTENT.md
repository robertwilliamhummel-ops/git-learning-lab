### 8. FAQ Section (Addresses Objections)
**Section Headline:** "Common Questions About SEO"

**Questions:**

**Q: How long does SEO take to work?**
A: Honest answer: 3-6 months for noticeable results, 6-12 months for significant impact. Anyone promising faster results is lying. SEO is a long-term investment, not a quick fix.

**Q: Can you guarantee #1 rankings?**
A: No, and neither can anyone else. Google's algorithm has over 200 ranking factors and changes constantly. We can improve your rankings significantly, but specific positions can't be guaranteed.

**Q: What's the difference between SEO and paid ads?**
A: SEO is long-term organic visibility (free clicks once you rank). Paid ads (Google Ads) give immediate visibility but cost per click. Most businesses benefit from both, but SEO provides better ROI long-term.

**Q: Do I need a new website for SEO?**
A: Not necessarily. If your existing site is technically sound, we can optimize it. However, if your site is outdated or poorly built, a redesign might be more cost-effective than extensive fixes.

**Q: How do you measure success?**
A: We track: keyword rankings, organic traffic growth, conversion rates, and ultimately—new customer inquiries. We provide transparent monthly reports showing exactly what's improving.

**Q: What if I'm not happy with the results?**
A: We offer month-to-month contracts (no long-term lock-ins). If you're not seeing progress after 3 months, you can cancel anytime. We're confident in our work and don't need to trap clients in contracts.
