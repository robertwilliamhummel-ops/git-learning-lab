**Goal:** Generate a professional cover photo for TechFlow Solutions Google Business Profile

## Variation 1: Original with Faded Skyline & Blue Text ⭐ (RECOMMENDED)

```
Create a professional Google Business Profile cover photo for TechFlow Solutions. Show a futuristic Toronto cityscape with CN Tower faded to 30-40% opacity in background - visible but not dominant, creating atmospheric depth. Overlay with holographic tech elements. LAYOUT FROM TOP TO BOTTOM: Position TWO-LINE text overlay in UPPER THIRD of image (about 1/3 from top), centered horizontally, in modern sans-serif font: Line 1 (LARGE, bold) in BRIGHT ACCENT BLUE (#4A90E2): "TechFlow Solutions" - make this very prominent and big. Line 2 (smaller, directly below) in WHITE: "Toronto Web Design & Tech Services" - about 50% size of line 1. BELOW the company text in CENTER/LOWER area: Three service icons arranged horizontally with SMALL TEXT LABELS underneath each icon: 1) Laptop icon with "Web Design" label below it, 2) Graph icon with "SEO" label below it, 3) Microchip icon with "Computer Repair" label below it - all icons glowing in bright purple gradient (#667eea to #764ba2), all small text labels in WHITE. Background: Dark navy with purple-blue gradient overlay. Toronto skyline should be subtle atmospheric element at 30-40% opacity. Include circuit board patterns and data streams connecting the service icons. Blue company name + white tagline + purple icons + white service labels creates perfect visual hierarchy. Style: Professional, service-focused, modern. CRITICAL: Company text in upper third, service icons WITH LABELS below in center.
```

**Why This Works:**
- ✅ Blue text (#4A90E2) contrasts beautifully with purple icons
- ✅ Toronto skyline at 30-40% opacity - visible but subtle
- ✅ Company name and services are the focus
- ✅ Skyline provides Toronto identity without distraction
- ✅ Perfect color balance: purple icons + blue text

## Usage Instructions:

1. **Try prompts in order** (1-20) or **pick based on style preference**

3. **Compare results** and choose the best one
4. **Save as:** `google-business-cover.png` or `cover-business-focused.png`
5. **Upload to Google Business Profile**

- **Professional/Clean:** Variations 2, 3, 11, 17
- **Futuristic/Tech:** Variations 4, 7, 10, 15
- **Toronto-Focused:** Variations 5, 16, 20
- **Abstract/Artistic:** Variations 4, 8, 18
- **Balanced/Structured:** Variations 9, 12, 14, 19

## Tips for Best Results:

- **Ideogram.ai** is best for text-heavy designs (Variations 1, 5, 10, 20)
- **Gemini** works well for abstract/artistic (Variations 4, 8, 18)
- **Simpler prompts** (Variations 2, 3, 17) often generate more consistently
- **Try the same prompt 3-5 times** - AI generates different results each time
