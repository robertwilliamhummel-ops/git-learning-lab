Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our remote support service

Is remote support secure?

Yes, absolutely. We use AnyDesk, which employs military-grade TLS 1.2 encryption and RSA 2048 asymmetric key exchange. All connections are secure and encrypted end-to-end. You maintain full control and can disconnect at any time. AnyDesk is trusted by millions of users worldwide and meets enterprise security standards.

Can you really install software and make system changes remotely?

Yes! Unlike Windows Quick Assist or Chrome Remote Desktop, AnyDesk provides full administrative access with UAC elevation. This means we can install software, update drivers, modify system settings, and perform complete repairs just as if we were sitting at your computer. There's virtually nothing we can't do remotely that we could do in person.

What if AnyDesk won't connect or download?

If you have trouble with AnyDesk, we can guide you through alternative connection methods over the phone or provide step-by-step troubleshooting. In rare cases where remote connection isn't possible due to severe system issues or network problems, we may recommend an on-site visit to resolve the underlying connectivity issue first.

How long do remote sessions typically take?

Most remote sessions take 1-2 hours, depending on the complexity of the issue. Simple problems like software installation or basic virus removal often take less than an hour, while comprehensive system optimization, major malware removal, or multiple software installations may take longer. We'll provide a time estimate after initial diagnosis.

Do I need to stay at my computer during the session?

For most of the session, you can step away while we work - many customers appreciate being able to do other things during the repair. However, we may need you available for initial setup, to approve certain system changes, provide passwords if needed, or to test functionality at the end of the session. We'll let you know when your input is required.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, credit cards, debit cards, and e-transfer. Payment is due upon completion of the remote session. We'll provide a detailed invoice of all work performed, including time spent and services rendered. For emergency support, payment may be required upfront.
