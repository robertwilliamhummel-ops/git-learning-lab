## ⚠️ Risk Assessment

### **Technical Infrastructure Risks: MINIMAL**

| Service | Uptime | Reliability | Risk Level |
|---------|--------|-------------|------------|
| **Firebase/Google Cloud** | 99.95%+ | Extremely high | ⚠️ MINIMAL |
| **Stripe Payments** | 99.99%+ | Industry-leading | ⚠️ MINIMAL |
| **Zoho/Gmail Email** | 99.9%+ | Very reliable | ⚠️ LOW |

**Firebase Reality:**
- Used by: Spotify, Duolingo, NY Times, Alibaba
- Downtime: ~4 hours per YEAR (usually minutes)
- Major outages: 3-4 in last 5 years
- When down: Back fast, rarely affects all regions
- **Bottom line:** Infrastructure is NOT your main risk

### **Real Risks & Mitigation:**

#### **1. Support Burden Risk (MODERATE)**

**Potential Issues:**
- "How do I edit a customer?" (rare - UI is simple)
- "I forgot my password" (occasional)
- "Can you add [feature]?" (scope creep - main danger)
- "My email went to spam" (email deliverability)

**Reality:**
- Initial training: 30-60 minutes
- First week questions: 1-2 calls/emails
- After that: 1-2 hours per client per YEAR
- Most clients need minimal support

**Mitigation:**
```
✅ Set Clear Boundaries:
   - Support hours: Mon-Fri, 9 AM - 5 PM
   - Response time: 24-48 hours
   - Scope: Bug fixes only, no free features
   - Emergency support: $200/hour extra

✅ Create Self-Service Resources:
   - Video tutorials (2-3 min each)
   - FAQ page
   - Written user guide
   - Troubleshooting checklist

✅ Charge for Extended Support:
   - Standard: 30 days included
   - Optional: $45/month ongoing
   - Additional: $50/hour as-needed
```

#### **2. Scope Creep Risk (MODERATE)**

**What Clients Might Ask:**
- "Can you add recurring invoices?"
- "Can you integrate with my accounting software?"
- "Can you add inventory tracking?"
- "Can you make it work offline?"

**Mitigation:**
```
✅ Define Clear Inclusions:
   What's Included:
   - Invoice creation & sending
   - Customer management
   - Payment links (Stripe)
   - PDF generation
   - Email automation
   - Standard support

   What's NOT Included:
   - Custom features
   - Third-party integrations
   - Major redesigns
   - Offline functionality
   - Data migration from other systems

✅ Charge for Custom Work:
   - Feature requests: $500-1,000 per feature
   - Custom integrations: $1,000-2,000
```
