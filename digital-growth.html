How long does SEO take to work?

Honest answer: 3-6 months for noticeable results, 6-12 months for significant impact. Anyone promising faster results is lying. SEO is a long-term investment, not a quick fix. Google needs time to crawl your site, index changes, and evaluate your improvements relative to competitors.

Can you guarantee #1 rankings?

No, and neither can anyone else. Google's algorithm has over 200 ranking factors and changes constantly. We can significantly improve your rankings, but specific positions can't be guaranteed. Anyone promising guaranteed #1 rankings is either lying or using risky black-hat tactics that will get you penalized.

What's the difference between SEO and paid ads?

SEO is long-term organic visibility (free clicks once you rank). Paid ads (Google Ads) give immediate visibility but cost per click. Most businesses benefit from both, but SEO provides better ROI long-term. Once you rank organically, those clicks are essentially free, whereas paid ads stop the moment you stop paying.

Do I need a new website for SEO?

Not necessarily. If your existing site is technically sound (fast, mobile-friendly, secure), we can optimize it. However, if your site is outdated, slow, or poorly built, a redesign might be more cost-effective than extensive fixes. We'll assess your site during the initial audit and give you an honest recommendation.

How do you measure success?

We track multiple metrics: keyword rankings, organic traffic growth, conversion rates, and ultimately—new customer inquiries. We provide transparent monthly reports showing exactly what's improving. Success isn't just about rankings; it's about driving qualified traffic that converts into paying customers.

What if I'm not happy with the results?

We offer month-to-month contracts (no long-term lock-ins). If you're not seeing progress after 3 months, you can cancel anytime. We're confident in our work and don't need to trap clients in contracts. Our goal is to earn your business every single month through results.
